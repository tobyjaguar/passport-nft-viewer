NFT Wrapper for Passport Prime

Builds the nft/ bundle the NFTs app on your Passport reads, and writes it straight onto the Passport's Airlock or a USB stick. Takes an address, never a key.

What leaves your browser. This page has no server. To find what the address holds it asks a public Blockscout explorer, and it downloads each image from wherever the NFT points (IPFS gateways, project CDNs). Those servers see your IP address and the address you enter — nothing else does. Read the source on GitHub.

1 What to fetch

Watch-only. Paste the address whose NFTs you want on the device, or scan it from a phone wallet.

The device caps a bundle at 50 items and 480 px per side; this page enforces the same limits.

    2 Put it on the Passport

    Pick the drive's root folder: AIRLOCK with the Passport plugged in, or your USB stick. The bundle goes into nft/; any older bundle there is replaced.

    Airlock is read-only until you allow writes on the device: Files → Airlock → ⋯ → Airlock Read & Write. It goes back to read-only when the cable comes out.

    For browsers without direct drive access (Firefox, Safari). Extract it onto the drive so nft/ sits at the top level.

    Scan address QR

    Point the camera at the wallet's address QR.

    Companion to passport-nft-viewer. Produces the same bundle as passport-nft-cli, the reference implementation, and the .raw writer is checked byte for byte against the SDK's foundation-asset-tool on every deploy. GPL-3.0-or-later.
